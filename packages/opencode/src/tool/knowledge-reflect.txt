Trigger a character-owned long-term knowledge update inside the roleplay world.

Use this tool when a character's stable understanding changes: about society, nature, their own body or senses, another person, a specific region, or a faction.

The Director may decide when knowledge handling should happen, but the character subagent must decide the final topic, wording, target file, and whether any update is warranted.

## Parameters

- `character`: The character whose long-term knowledge may need revision.
- `situation`: A concise current-scene summary from that character's perspective.
- `event`: The concrete event, realization, or repeated pattern that may change the character's durable understanding.
- `guidance` (optional): Extra Director guidance such as "relationship re-evaluation", "new natural pattern learned", or "social judgment changed".

## Knowledge File Directory Contract

Files under `{character}/knowledge/` hold character-owned durable understanding:

- `world_base.yaml`: Overall worldview and natural laws — broad, general rules about how the world works.
- `social_and_world.md`: Overall social landscape, political culture, and societal patterns — broad observations about nations, cultures, or the world as a whole.
- `nature_and_body.md`: Natural environment and body/sensory experience — climate, terrain, physical habits, sensory patterns.
- `<person_or_entity>.md`: Knowledge about a **specific** person, region, faction, or entity. Create separate files for specific targets.
- Other custom files may exist for specialized topics.

When updating knowledge:

- Write to `world_base.yaml`, `social_and_world.md`, or `nature_and_body.md` only for **broad, general** observations about the world, society, or nature.
- For knowledge about a **specific** person, region, faction, or entity, create or update a dedicated file (e.g., `li_ming.md`, `northern_frontier.md`, `iron_sect.md`).
- To update your own stats, abilities, history, identity, or beliefs, use `profile.yaml` — not knowledge files.

## Important

- This is not for public records. Use `records/` for public world history.
- The runtime will dispatch a character subagent session and expose only the character's own read/write knowledge tools plus memory access.
- The character, not the Director, owns the final contents of their directory-based knowledge files.
