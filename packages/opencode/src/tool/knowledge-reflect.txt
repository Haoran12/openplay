Trigger a character-owned long-term knowledge update inside the roleplay world.

Use this tool when a character's stable understanding changes: about society, nature, their own body or senses, or another person.

The Director may decide when knowledge handling should happen, but the character subagent must decide the final topic, wording, target file, and whether any update is warranted.

## Parameters

- `character`: The character whose long-term knowledge may need revision.
- `situation`: A concise current-scene summary from that character's perspective.
- `event`: The concrete event, realization, or repeated pattern that may change the character's durable understanding.
- `guidance` (optional): Extra Director guidance such as "relationship re-evaluation", "new natural pattern learned", or "social judgment changed".

## Important

- This is not for public records. Use `records/` for public world history.
- The runtime will dispatch a character subagent session and expose only the character's own read/write knowledge tools plus memory access.
- The character, not the Director, owns the final contents of their directory-based knowledge files.
