Trigger a character-owned long-term knowledge update inside the roleplay world.

Use this tool when a character's stable understanding changes: about society, nature, their own body or senses, another person, a specific region, a faction, or a social phenomenon.

The Director may decide when knowledge handling should happen, but the character subagent must decide the final topic, wording, target file, and whether any update is warranted.

## Parameters

- `character`: The character whose long-term knowledge may need revision.
- `situation`: A concise current-scene summary from that character's perspective.
- `event`: The concrete event, realization, or repeated pattern that may change the character's durable understanding.
- `guidance` (optional): Extra Director guidance such as "relationship re-evaluation", "new natural pattern learned", or "social judgment changed".

## Knowledge File Directory Contract

Files under `{character}/knowledge/` hold character-owned durable understanding:

- `world_base.yaml`: Overall worldview and natural laws — broad, general rules.
- `social_and_world.md`: Overall social landscape and political culture.
- `nature_and_body.md`: Natural environment and body/sensory experience.
- `<name>.md`: Knowledge about a **specific** person, region, faction, or social phenomenon.

**Self vs Others:**

- `profile.yaml`: Your own attributes, abilities, experience, mindModel, role, appearance.
- `knowledge/<name>.md`: Knowledge about **others** (specific persons, regions, factions).

**Before creating a new file:**

Check existing files to avoid duplicates (e.g., if `li_ming.md` exists, update it instead of creating `李明.md`).

When updating knowledge:

- Write to `world_base.yaml`/`social_and_world.md`/`nature_and_body.md` only for **broad, general** observations.
- For **specific** persons, regions, factions, or social phenomena, create or update dedicated files.

## Important

- This is not for public records. Use `records/` for public world history.
- The runtime will dispatch a character subagent session and expose only the character's own read/write knowledge tools plus memory access.
- The character, not the Director, owns the final contents of their directory-based knowledge files.
